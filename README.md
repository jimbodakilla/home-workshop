# Home Workshop

A small daily workshop page for a young child, built to run on an iPad or phone with no account and nothing to install.

A friendly robot helper lives on a pegboard wall and offers four stations: **Read**, **Build**, **Explain** and **Numbers**. Each gives three choices, then one short how-to with steps the child can tap to tick off. Finishing a station earns a star toward a weekly treasure and a **sticker** for the album. There are 24 stickers to find, and finding one again upgrades it to silver, then gold. There are 77 activities, shuffled so the same three choices never come back day after day.

- Everything is ordinary reading, vocabulary, building and math. There are no test-style questions, and nothing is ever locked or marked wrong.
- Nothing leaves the device. Names, progress and notes are kept in the browser's local storage only, and a grown-up can copy a backup and restore it on another device.
- Setup is one friendly question at a time. A press-and-hold gate keeps the grown-up area (tabs for today, records, settings and backup) away from the child.
- Works offline after the first visit and can be added to the Home Screen. Read-aloud, a visual timer and sound are all optional. Motion is slow and sparing, and switches off entirely for reduced-motion settings.

One HTML file plus a small service worker. No build step, no tracking, no dependencies except the two Google fonts, which are kept after the first visit.
