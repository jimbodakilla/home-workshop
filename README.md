# Home Workshop

A daily learning workshop for a young child, built to run on an iPad or phone with no account and nothing to install. **Everything is played on the screen.** Every story, lesson, picture, puzzle and question is inside the page. Nothing sends the child off to find a book or gather things.

A friendly robot helper lives on a pegboard wall and offers four stations, each with several short activities of about five minutes:

- **Read**: Story Time (illustrated picture stories, then questions), Word Detective (work out a new word from the sentence), Word Builder (word parts and compound words), Opposites and Matches.
- **Build**: Bolt's Path (program the robot), Maze Runner, Mirror Magic (symmetry), Logic Clues (who has what), Invention Lab (think up new uses, no wrong answers).
- **Explain**: Picture Lessons (a whole lesson drawn page by page, then questions), True or Tricky, Why Does It?
- **Numbers**: Block Builder (place value), Quick Sums, Groups and Arrays, Fraction Pizza, Clock Time, Story Math.

Every activity adapts. It starts at about grade 2, moves up when the child gets most answers right first time, and eases back when it is too hard. There is a free hint, one gentle retry, then the answer with the reason. Nothing is timed and nothing is marked in red. Finishing a round earns a star toward a weekly treasure and a **sticker** (24 to find, upgrading to silver and gold). A Word Jar collects the words learned.

- A press-and-hold gate keeps the **grown-up area** away from the child. Its Progress tab shows what the child can do in each activity, and copies a plain record that says clearly it is practice results, not standardized test scores.
- Nothing leaves the device. Names, progress and notes are kept in the browser's local storage only, and a grown-up can copy a backup and restore it on another device.
- Works offline after the first visit and can be added to the Home Screen. Read-aloud and sound are optional. Motion is slow and sparing, and switches off for reduced-motion settings.

One HTML file plus a small service worker. No build step, no tracking, no dependencies except the two Google fonts, which are kept after the first visit.
